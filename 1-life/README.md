# 1. Life

> **Only you** — never shared. No grant can name this folder.

Your life outside work: what happens, the people in it, your health, your goals, and what you make of it over time.

1. [Journal](1-journal/README.md) — dated entries and daily check-ins
2. [People](2-people/README.md) — one page per person
3. [Health](3-health/README.md) — sealed: accepts no writes until sealing is available
4. [Goals](4-goals/README.md) — one page per goal, with where it stands
5. Inner work — an opt-in folder, off by default (`inner_work` in [life-config.yaml](../0-meta/life-config.yaml)); created sealed when you turn it on
6. [Reviews](6-reviews/README.md) — weekly and quarterly reviews
7. [Synthesis](7-synthesis/README.md) — patterns and themes drawn across entries
8. [Sources](8-sources/README.md) — raw inputs, kept as they arrived
